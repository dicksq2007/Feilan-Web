Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are

Feilan Global (“Feilan”, “we”, “us”) is operated by Hangzhou Feilan Global Travel Co., Ltd., registered at No. 189 Tongbei Road, Ningwei Street, Xiaoshan District, Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province, China. We provide medical information consultation and medical coordination services only. We are not a medical institution and do not provide diagnosis, treatment or prescriptions. Contact: contact@flgj.net.

2. Information We Collect

Information you submit through forms. Our “Free Consultation” and “Partnership Inquiry” forms collect your name, email address, treatment interest, expected travel time, health-related details you choose to share, and your messages. The patient form includes an explicit consent checkbox for processing health information, which you must tick before submission.

Health information. Only the information you voluntarily provide for medical coordination (e.g., symptoms, medical history in the message fields) is collected. We never request more health data than necessary for coordination purposes.

Analytics data. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookie-less analytics service. It does not use cookies, does not collect personal identifiers, and requires no consent under GDPR.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to: respond to your inquiry within 24 hours; coordinate consultations, pre-screening and quotes with partner medical institutions; arrange travel, accommodation, interpretation and insurance services you request; comply with legal obligations. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Processors

FormSubmit (Devro LABS). Form submissions are forwarded to our mailbox via FormSubmit (https://formsubmit.co), which acts solely as a data processor for transmission. FormSubmit's servers are located in the United States, so your form data will be transferred outside your country of residence for processing. By submitting a form, you acknowledge and consent to this cross-border transfer.

Partner medical institutions. With your consent, we share the minimum necessary information with the hospitals and physicians who independently provide medical services and bear medical responsibility.

Cloudflare. Our website is hosted and delivered via Cloudflare infrastructure, which may process limited technical data (IP address, browser type) for security and delivery purposes.

5. Cross-Border Data Transfers

As a cross-border medical coordination platform, your information may be transferred between China, the United States, the European Union and your country of residence. We implement contractual and technical safeguards consistent with China’s PIPL, the EU GDPR and the US HIPAA framework for health information, and we transfer only the minimum data necessary for the stated purposes.

6. Health Data Protection

Health information receives heightened protection. We process it only with your explicit consent, restrict access to personnel and coordinators who need it, store it encrypted, and retain it only for the period necessary for your care coordination and follow-up services. You may request deletion of your health information at any time (see Section 9).

7. Cookies and Consent

We use strictly necessary cookies to run this site. Analytics is cookie-less (Cloudflare Web Analytics) and requires no consent. Marketing cookies are only set with your permission. Your choice is recorded locally in your browser and can be changed anytime via the cookie banner preferences.

8. Data Retention

Form submissions and related correspondence are retained only as long as necessary to handle your inquiry, provide follow-up services, and meet legal or accounting requirements, after which they are securely deleted or anonymized.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to withdraw your consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal), and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, email contact@flgj.net. We will respond within 15 business days.

10. Security

We apply industry-standard technical and organizational measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit, access controls, and processor due diligence. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we will notify you and relevant authorities promptly in the event of a confirmed data breach as required by law.

11. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the latest version. Material changes will be announced on this page before taking effect.

13. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, requests or complaints: contact@flgj.net, or write to Hangzhou Feilan Global Travel Co., Ltd., No. 189 Tongbei Road, Ningwei Street, Xiaoshan District, Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province, China.

隐私政策

一、我们是谁

飞澜国际（以下简称“我们”）由杭州飞澜国际旅游有限公司运营，注册地址为中国浙江省杭州市萧山区宁围街道通北路189号。我们仅提供医疗信息咨询与就医协调服务，并非医疗机构，不提供医学诊断、治疗或处方。联系方式：contact@flgj.net。

二、我们收集哪些信息

您通过表单提交的信息。“预约免费咨询”与“商务合作”表单会收集您的姓名、电子邮箱、意向诊疗项目、预计出行时间、您主动填写的健康相关信息及留言。患者表单设有健康信息处理同意复选框，必须勾选后方可提交。

健康信息。仅收集您为就医协调目的自愿提供的信息（如留言中描述的症状、病史），我们不会要求超出协调所必需范围的健康数据。

分析数据。我们使用 Cloudflare Web Analytics 无 Cookie 分析服务，不收集个人标识信息，无需另行取得同意。

三、我们如何使用您的信息

我们仅在以下场景使用所收集的信息：在 24 小时内回复您的咨询；与合作医疗机构协调会诊、预筛与报价；安排您要求的旅行、住宿、翻译与保险服务；履行法定义务。我们不会将您的个人信息出售、出租或交易给任何第三方用于营销目的。

四、第三方处理服务

FormSubmit（Devro LABS 运营）。表单信息经由 FormSubmit（https://formsubmit.co）转发至我们的邮箱，其仅作为传输目的的数据处理方。FormSubmit 服务器位于美国，您的表单数据将被跨境传输处理。提交表单即表示您知悉并同意上述跨境传输安排。

合作医疗机构。经您同意后，我们仅向独立提供医疗服务并承担医疗责任的医院及医师共享必要的信息。

Cloudflare。本网站由 Cloudflare 基础设施托管与分发，其可能为安全与交付目的处理有限的技术数据（如 IP 地址、浏览器类型）。

五、跨境数据传输

作为跨境医疗协调平台，您的信息可能在中国、美国、欧盟及您所在国家/地区之间传输。我们按照中国《个人信息保护法》（PIPL）、欧盟 GDPR 及美国 HIPAA 健康信息框架实施合同与技术保障措施，仅传输实现上述目的所必需的最少数据。

六、健康信息的特别保护

健康信息受到更高标准保护：我们仅在取得您的明确同意后处理，限制接触人员范围，加密存储，并仅在照护协调与随访服务所必需的期限内保留。您可随时要求删除您的健康信息（见第九条）。

七、Cookie 与同意

我们仅使用严格必要的 Cookie 运行本站；分析服务为无 Cookie 方案（Cloudflare Web Analytics），无需同意；营销类 Cookie 仅在您许可后启用。您的选择记录在浏览器本地，可随时通过 Cookie 横幅偏好设置更改。

八、信息留存期限

表单提交信息及相关通信记录仅在处理您的咨询、提供后续服务及满足法律或会计要求的必要期限内保留，到期后将被安全删除或匿名化。

九、您的权利

您有权访问、更正、删除、限制或反对处理您的个人信息，有权随时撤回同意（不影响撤回前处理的合法性），并有权向监管机构投诉。如需行使上述权利，请发送邮件至 contact@flgj.net，我们将在 15 个工作日内回复。

十、信息安全

我们采取行业标准的技术与管理措施，包括传输全程 HTTPS 加密、访问权限控制及处理方尽职调查。任何传输与存储方式均无法保证百分之百安全；如发生经确认的数据泄露，我们将依法及时通知您及相关监管机构。

十一、未成年人保护

我们的服务不面向 16 岁以下未成年人。我们不会主动收集未成年人的个人信息。如您发现未成年人向我们提交了信息，请联系我们删除。

十二、本政策的更新

我们可能不时更新本政策，页首“最近更新”日期即最新版本。重大变更将在生效前于本页面公布。

十三、联系我们

如有任何与隐私相关的问题、请求或投诉，请联系：contact@flgj.net，或致函：杭州飞澜国际旅游有限公司，中国浙江省杭州市萧山区宁围街道通北路189号。
