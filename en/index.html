Medical Tourism

Journeys of Healing: Discover China on Your Way to Recovery

Where world-class care meets world-class travel — alongside your treatment, our professional team accompanies you through the most celebrated landscapes of Shanghai, Zhejiang and Yunnan. Expand each region to explore its TOP 10 attractions and care pairings.

ShanghaiTOP 10

China's economic and cultural heart, where East meets West. Historic Bund facades face the futuristic Lujiazui skyline across the Huangpu River, and shikumen lanes live on beside gleaming skyscrapers.

+
The BundA gallery of global architecture on the Huangpu waterfront +

Stretching 1.5 km along the Huangpu River, the Bund is lined with 52 grand buildings in Gothic, Baroque and Neoclassical styles — an open-air museum of the city's cosmopolitan past, facing the futuristic Lujiazui skyline.

Pair with：Treatment at a Grade 3A hospital International Medical Department + a sunset riverside recovery walk

Yu Garden & City God TempleA Ming-dynasty classical garden in the heart of old Shanghai +

Built in the Ming dynasty, Yu Garden is Shanghai's only surviving classical garden of winding corridors, rockeries and koi ponds. The surrounding bazaar teems with time-honored eateries and traditional crafts.

Pair with：A gentle half-day garden stroll after your health check-up

Oriental Pearl TV TowerShanghai's iconic tower with 360° views over both banks +

Rising 468 meters in Lujiazui, the Oriental Pearl was China's tallest tower when completed. Its observation decks and glass skywalk offer a full panorama of the river and skyline.

Pair with：Light-activity sightseeing during recovery from ophthalmology or ENT procedures

Shanghai MuseumOne of China's finest museums of ancient art +

Housing over a million objects, Shanghai Museum is celebrated worldwide for its bronzes, ceramics, paintings and jade. Its round-top, square-base design itself symbolizes the ancient cosmos.

Pair with：An indoor cultural visit between appointments — perfect in any season

TianzifangAn artsy labyrinth of shikumen lanes +

Converted from century-old shikumen lanes, Tianzifang's alleys are packed with galleries, studios, cafés and boutiques — the most charming corner of Shanghai's creative scene.

Pair with：A short walking tour between dental treatment sessions

Nanjing Road Pedestrian StreetChina's No.1 commercial street, glittering with neon +

Shanghai's busiest shopping street mixes century-old stores with flagship boutiques. After dark its neon lights up the boulevard — an easy, level route with sightseeing trains available.

Pair with：A flat, relaxed walking itinerary during post-surgical recovery

XintiandiWhere shikumen heritage meets modern lifestyle +

Xintiandi restored entire blocks of 1920s shikumen houses into a stylish district of restaurants, cafés and boutiques — the signature blend of heritage and nightlife.

Pair with：An ideal venue for business receptions and partnership dinners

Wukang Road Historic AreaAn open-air museum of century-old villas under plane trees +

Just over a kilometer long, Wukang Road gathers 37 protected historic buildings including the famed Wukang Mansion — its French and Spanish villas beneath plane trees make Shanghai's most elegant city walk.

Pair with：A low-intensity afternoon walk after your check-up

Shanghai TowerChina's tallest building — the city at cloud level +

At 632 meters, Shanghai Tower is China's tallest and the world's third-tallest skyscraper. The 'Top of Shanghai' observatory on the 118th floor is reached in 55 seconds by one of the world's fastest elevators.

Pair with：City sightseeing for guests in good physical condition

Zhujiajiao Water TownShanghai's 'Venice' — 1,700 years of canal town heritage +

Nicknamed Shanghai's Venice, Zhujiajiao has grown along its canals for 1,700 years, with 36 stone bridges including the city's largest. A rowing-boat ride reveals classic Jiangnan water-town life.

Pair with：A weekend water-town getaway for rest and recuperation

ZhejiangTOP 10

The essence of the Jiangnan water country and the heartland of China's digital economy. From the poetic West Lake to Wuzhen's canals, from sacred Mount Putuo to Yandang's peaks, landscape and heritage weave into one.

+
West LakeA UNESCO World Heritage 'paradise on earth' +

Immortalized by poets as 'paradise on earth', West Lake is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Its Ten Scenes shift with the seasons — strolling the Su Causeway or cruising the lake is pure tranquility.

Pair with：Our signature premium check-up + lakeside wellness walk

Lingyin Temple & Feilai FengA 1,700-year-old Zen sanctuary by West Lake +

Founded in the Eastern Jin dynasty over 1,700 years ago, Lingyin Temple is one of China's most important Chan Buddhist temples. The Feilai Feng grottoes preserve exquisite Song-Yuan carvings amid ancient trees.

Pair with：TCM wellness conditioning + a mindfulness retreat

Wuzhen Water TownChina's best-preserved water town +

Preserving its late-Qing streetscape intact, Wuzhen's cross-shaped canals divide the town into quarters of whitewashed houses and swaying wooden boats — famed as 'China's last riverside home'.

Pair with：A slow-living convalescent stay by the water

Xitang Ancient TownA thousand-year-old "living" water town +

A thousand years old, Xitang is famed for its many bridges, lanes and covered riverside corridors. The one-kilometer Misty Rain Corridor keeps strollers dry in any weather — a living picture of Jiangnan life.

Pair with：A photogenic retreat for wellness travelers

Mount PutuoA sacred Buddhist island in the East China Sea +

One of China's four sacred Buddhist mountains and the bodhimanda of Guanyin, Mount Putuo rises from the sea with ancient temples, golden beaches and a towering Guanyin statue — a sanctuary for body and mind.

Pair with：A spiritual healing journey + seaside air therapy

Qiandao Lake1,078 emerald islands on crystal-clear water +

Formed by the Xin'an River hydropower project, Qiandao Lake scatters 1,078 green islands across water of national first-class quality — cycling, boating and fresh fish cuisine make it a natural wellness hotspot.

Pair with：Post-operative recovery in a forest-oxygen setting

Yandang MountainA UNESCO Global Geopark of peaks and waterfalls +

Shaped by volcanic eruptions 120 million years ago, Yandang Mountain is celebrated for dramatic peaks, caves and the 197-meter Dalongqiu Waterfall — the 'No.1 waterfall under heaven'.

Pair with：Low-altitude hiking routes suitable for orthopedic recovery (with physician approval)

MoganshanA century-old hill resort and China's boutique-stay capital +

Famed for bamboo forests, sea of clouds and springs, Moganshan has been a summer retreat since the late Qing, dotted with 200 historic villas — today the birthplace of China's boutique guesthouse culture.

Pair with：A premium package of TCM therapy + mountain retreat

Hengdian World StudiosThe world's largest film studio — China's Hollywood +

The world's largest film and TV production base, Hengdian features a dozen full-scale sets spanning millennia — from Qin-dynasty palaces to Song streetscapes — a walk through cinematic history.

Pair with：Entertainment for accompanying family while the patient rests

Qiantang River Tidal BoreThe world's most spectacular tidal bore +

The world's greatest tidal bore peaks around the 18th day of the eighth lunar month, when single-line, crossing and returning tides surge up the estuary — a spectacle famed since antiquity.

Pair with：A seasonal natural wonder for autumn medical travelers

YunnanTOP 10

China's most culturally colorful province — a kingdom of plants and wildlife. Snow mountains, lakes, rice terraces, rainforests and ancient towns unfold under an eternal-spring climate: an ideal land for wellness travel.

+
Lijiang Old TownA UNESCO-listed Naxi gem beneath the snow mountains +

Founded in the late Song dynasty, Lijiang preserves China's most complete minority old-town fabric — a UNESCO site since 1997. Canals wind past wooden houses around Sifang Square, with Naxi music in the air.

Pair with：Plateau-climate wellness stays (≈2,400 m altitude — subject to medical advice)

Jade Dragon Snow MountainA sacred snow range with turquoise alpine lakes +

Thirteen peaks of Jade Dragon keep snow year-round above mirror-blue waters. A cable car reaches the 4,506 m glacier park for close views of the 5,596 m summit, sacred to the Naxi people.

Pair with：Snow-mountain sightseeing for guests with good cardiopulmonary fitness (medical assessment required)

Erhai LakeDali's 'highland pearl' between mountain and town +

Shaped like an ear, Erhai is Yunnan's second-largest highland lake. Cycle or cruise its shores for the classic wind-flower-snow-moon vistas, with Bai villages along the water.

Pair with：Gentle lakeside cycling routes during post-operative recovery

Stone Forest (Shilin)A 270-million-year-old karst wonder, UNESCO Natural Heritage +

Carved over 270 million years, Shilin is the world's textbook example of karst pinnacles and a UNESCO Natural Heritage Site — a grey limestone 'forest' of towers and corridors that astounds every visitor.

Pair with：A half-day natural wonder tour for guests treated in Kunming

Pudacuo National ParkShangri-La's alpine lake and forest sanctuary +

Mainland China's first national park, Pudacuo mirrors the sky in alpine lakes ringed by primeval forest and meadows — ablaze with azaleas in spring, 'the closest place to heaven'.

Pair with：Forest-bathing wellness therapy (above 3,500 m — subject to medical advice)

Lugu LakeA crystal 'highland pearl' of the Mosuo people +

At 2,690 m, Lugu Lake's waters are famously limpid, home to the matrilineal Mosuo community. Ride a pig-trough canoe, cross the Walking-Marriage Bridge and sleep beneath starry skies.

Pair with：A quiet convalescent retreat + minority culture immersion

Three Pagodas of Chongsheng TempleDali's thousand-year landmark mirrored in the lake +

Built in the Nanzhao kingdom, the Three Pagodas have watched over Dali for a millennium; the 69-meter Qianxun Pagoda is among China's tallest surviving ancient towers, mirrored famously in the park lake.

Pair with：A cultural mindfulness stop on a Dali wellness itinerary

Yuanyang Rice TerracesA millennium of Hani farming sculpted into the mountains +

Hani farmers carved these terraces over 1,300 years — a UNESCO Cultural Landscape. In irrigation season the paddies mirror the sky in endless tiers, glowing at sunrise like sculptures of the earth.

Pair with：A winter photography-focused wellness tour

XishuangbannaChina's only tropical rainforest kingdom +

Home to China's only tropical-rainforest reserve, Xishuangbanna offers sky-tree canopy walks, wild elephants and the Dai Water-Splashing Festival — the perfect winter escape.

Pair with：Winter warm-weather recuperation + TCM seasonal conditioning

Tiger Leaping GorgeOne of the world's deepest gorges on the Jinsha River +

Where the Jinsha River thunders between Jade Dragon and Haba snow mountains, Tiger Leaping Gorge plunges 3,900 m — among the deepest canyons on earth and a world-class trekking route.

Pair with：Light trekking only for guests in excellent health (professional assessment required)

* Every itinerary is flexibly tailored to your treatment plan and physical condition, accompanied throughout by licensed guides and medically trained escorts. Plateau and trekking programs require physician approval.

FAQ

Answers to Your Questions

Frequent questions about visas, language, pricing and aftercare. Can’t find your answer? Feel free to contact us.

Do I need a visa for China?
China offers 240-hour visa-free transit for travelers from 55 countries, which covers most medical travelers. Our advisors help confirm your eligibility and plan your stay; if a visa is required, we provide supporting materials.
What language do my medical records need to be in? Do I translate them myself?
Not at all. After you submit an inquiry, your dedicated advisor helps collect your records, and our medically trained team translates and forwards them to partner hospitals for pre-screening.
How do I pay for treatment? Are there any hidden fees?
Hospital fees are charged directly by the hospital according to its official written quote; our service fee is fixed at signing. Any plan changes require your prior approval — there are no hidden fees. International cards and bank transfers are accepted; insured guests may use direct billing.
I don’t speak Chinese — how will I communicate during treatment?
Worry-free throughout. A medically trained student escort provides one-on-one translation for registration, consultations, examinations, prescriptions and hospitalization, plus bilingual report interpretation.
What happens after I fly home — how do I get follow-up care?
Members enjoy post-operative remote follow-up, check-up reminders and rehabilitation guidance. When needed, we help your Chinese physicians communicate with your local doctors. Your health records continue across borders, compliantly.
Are the prices shown on the website final?
All figures on this site are general descriptions. Typical cost examples are for reference only — final pricing is confirmed in the hospital’s official written quote. After you inquire, an advisor provides tailored cost references within 24 hours.
How is my health data protected?
We process your health information under triple compliance — China PIPL, EU GDPR and US HIPAA — use it only to the extent necessary for medical coordination, and store it encrypted. Please see our Privacy Policy for details.
How long does the whole process take, from inquiry to treatment in China?
Light medical services (check-ups, dental, ophthalmology) can usually be arranged within 2–4 weeks; major surgery depends on specialist scheduling and pre-screening. Your advisor provides a clear timeline after signing.
